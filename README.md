# Ethereum EIPs & ERCs

A personal collection of Ethereum standards and improvement proposals that catch my attention during my journey as a smart contract developer.

This repository is where I keep track of the ideas, designs, and technical proposals I find worth exploring. I add the ones that interest me, study how they work, and document the concepts and insights I want to revisit.

## Start Here

New to Ethereum standards? Begin with the [EIPs and ERCs: A Developer's Guide](00-Introduction/what-are-eips-and-ercs.md), which explains the proposal framework, lifecycle, standards categories, and how to evaluate standards as a developer.

## Repository Structure

The numbered folders are arranged to make the repository easy to navigate, with the introduction first and the main collections grouped afterward.

- **[00 — Introduction](00-Introduction/)** — Start here to understand EIPs, ERCs, their lifecycle, and how to evaluate standards.
- **[01 — EIPs](01-EIPs/)** — Selected protocol, networking, interface, and other improvement proposals worth studying.
- **[02 — ERCs](02-ERCs/)** — Application-layer standards, contract interfaces, and interoperability conventions.
- **[03 — Notes](03-Notes/)** — Cross-cutting research notes, comparisons, design observations, and implementation learnings.

## What You'll Find Here

- **Interesting Standards** — ERCs and other standards that I find technically valuable.
- **Improvement Proposals** — EIPs proposing changes or new ideas for Ethereum.
- **Technical Notes** — My personal understanding of how selected proposals work and why they matter.
- **Things Worth Exploring** — Designs, mechanisms, and ideas I want to understand more deeply.

## My Approach

This is not intended to be a complete list of Ethereum standards. It is a personal, evolving collection shaped by my interests, research, and learning.

I focus on understanding the ideas behind each proposal, the problems it addresses, its potential applications, and the trade-offs involved.

Official specifications and proposal statuses are linked to their original sources.
